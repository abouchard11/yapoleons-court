# Yapoleon's Court — Brand Guidelines

**Status:** v1.0 · 2026-06-15 · derived from the LOCKED design ([DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md)) and the live tokens in [src/index.css](../../src/index.css).
**Operator:** MidnightDev (midnightdev.dev). Second game in the **Yapoleon** franchise (sibling to *yapword*) — share the character, distinguish the game.

This is the single source of truth for the public-facing identity (favicon, app icon, social card, legal pages). The in-app UI contract lives separately in the phase `UI-SPEC.md`; this doc governs the **brand marks and shell assets**.

---

## 1. Essence

Yapoleon is a haughty, impossible-to-please **AI emperor**. You win his *favor* by charming him with a single witty reply — **his taste is the scoring function.** The brand should feel **regal, dry-witted, and exacting**: an imperial court rendered with restraint, not a cartoon king. Register echoes the voice: Wilde/Twain, ego-is-the-joke, PG savagery aimed at the *wit*, never the person.

**Three words:** Imperial · Witty · Exacting.

## 2. The Mark

The franchise-distinct mark is a **gold imperial crown over a gold "favor meter" bar, on imperial navy** — distinct from yapword's "Y" monogram while reading as the same family (navy tile + gold accent + the gold bar).

- **Construction** (canonical crown path, normalised to a ~100×72 box):
  `M6 72 L6 52 L18 10 L34 44 L50 2 L66 44 L82 10 L94 52 L94 72 Z` in gold `#E8B84B`, with finial orbs at the three tips `(18,10) r6 · (50,3) r7 · (82,10) r6` and a **crimson `#C8302A` gem** centred on the band `(50,60) r6`. The favor-meter bar sits below the crown.
- **Source files:** [public/favicon.svg](../../public/favicon.svg) (rounded-tile, the browser mark) and [scripts/assets/app-icon.svg](../../scripts/assets/app-icon.svg) (full-bleed maskable, the app/PWA icon). PNG/ICO sizes are generated by [scripts/gen-public-assets.sh](../../scripts/gen-public-assets.sh).
- **Clear space:** keep a margin of at least the crown-band height (≈ one favor-bar height) on all sides. On the rounded tile this is the navy field between the mark and the corner radius.
- **Minimum size:** the full lockup (crown + favor bar) holds to **24 px**; the crown silhouette alone is legible to **16 px** (verified). Below ~24 px, use **crown-only** — the favor bar merges into a line.
- **Color variants:**
  - *Primary* — gold crown + crimson gem on navy.
  - *Maskable* — full-bleed navy, no rounded corners, mark inside the centre ~60% safe zone ([scripts/assets/app-icon.svg](../../scripts/assets/app-icon.svg)).
  - *Cream / light field* — on parchment, set the crown inside a **navy roundel** (gold crown, crimson gem); never place the gold mark directly on bare cream (contrast). See the identity board, [docs/brand/brand-board-identity.png](brand-board-identity.png).
  - *Knockout / mono* — single-color, **gem dropped**: ink `#1A1714` on light, cream `#FBF6EC` on dark/photo, gold `#E8B84B` on navy. Recolorable master [scripts/assets/mark-mono.svg](../../scripts/assets/mark-mono.svg) (`fill=currentColor`) → [mark-mono-ink.png](mark-mono-ink.png) / [mark-mono-cream.png](mark-mono-cream.png) / [mark-mono-gold.png](mark-mono-gold.png) via [scripts/gen-brand-marks.sh](../../scripts/gen-brand-marks.sh). See [mark-mono-preview.png](mark-mono-preview.png).
- **Misuse:** don't recolor the crown outside the palette; don't add gradients/bevels/shadows; don't stretch; don't place the gold mark on cream without the navy tile/roundel (insufficient contrast); don't rotate.

## 3. Color

The five tokens are defined as CSS variables in [src/index.css:5-13](../../src/index.css) — **treat these hexes as exact.**

| Token | Hex | CSS var | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Navy** | `#1B2A4A` | `--yap-navy` | Primary / imperial field, tiles, dark UI chrome |
| **Gold** | `#E8B84B` | `--yap-gold` | Primary accent — the crown, the favor meter, rules |

**Supporting (derived, for depth):** darker-gold `#C8902B` (links/pressed gold), highlight-gold `#F2D07E` (top-light on gold), navy-light `#24365A` (meter track, raised navy).

**Appearance:** the app ships a light **cream** default and a dark **`#1C1C1E`** mode (`html.dark`, [src/index.css:154-175](../../src/index.css)); `theme-color` resolves to `#FBF6EC` (light) / `#1C1C1E` (dark) at runtime. The manifest `background_color`/`theme_color` are cream `#FBF6EC`.

## 4. Typography

- **Display:** **Bricolage Grotesque** Bold (700) — a heavy humanist grotesque. Used for the wordmark, headings, big numerals. `font-family: 'Bricolage Grotesque'` ([tailwind](../../tailwind.config.js) `font-display`).
- **Body / UI:** **Inter** (400–700).
- **Self-hosted**, no CDN: [public/fonts/BricolageGrotesque-Bold.woff2](../../public/fonts/) and `Inter-Latin.woff2`, declared via `@font-face` in [src/index.css:83-116](../../src/index.css) with `font-display: swap`. The same two faces style the standalone legal pages.
- **Rasterized assets** (og-image, favicon): because Bricolage isn't installed for the SVG rasterizer, baked images use a heavy system stand-in (`Helvetica Neue` Bold). Spec the wordmark as Bricolage; accept the stand-in only in pre-rendered raster.

## 5. Texture & signature device

- **Dotted parchment grid** — a 22 px radial-dot grid is the franchise's "hand-crafted paper" texture: ink dots at ~6% on cream, gold dots at ~6–7% on navy ([src/index.css:22-23,160-162](../../src/index.css)). Use it as a quiet field, never loud.
- **The favor meter (0–100)** is both the core game mechanic *and* a brand device: a gold-filled rounded bar on a navy-light track. Reach for it as the signature visual on cards and boards — a partially-filled meter says "win his favor" without words.

### 5.1 Favor meter — canonical component

One meter, used consistently:

- **Track** `#24365A` (navy-light) · **Fill** `#E8B84B` (gold) · fully pill-rounded (radius = ½ height) · scale **0–100**.
- **The numeric readout must equal the fill.** If a value is shown, fill the bar to that percentage (e.g. `78` ⇒ 78% filled — the system board). A mismatch (a 78%-filled bar labelled "100") is a defect.
- **Goal end** may carry a small gold **crown marker** = favor won at 100; the meter visibly *fills toward the crown* (the OG card).
- In the **logo**, the bar is a solid gold plinth — a base element, not a live state. Only **UI and cards** show a partial fill.

## 7. Voice in the visuals

Yapoleon's lines may appear as **sparse, confident text** — a single regal phrase, never a paragraph. Approved short forms: *"Charm the AI emperor." · "His taste is the scoring function." · "Win his favor in three lines."* Savagery, when shown, targets the cleverness of a reply, never a person or a protected trait (the all-ages output bound). Keep type minimal and let navy/gold/the-crown do the talking.

---

## 8. Do / Don't

**Do** — use the exact five hexes · let navy + gold carry it · keep crimson rare · keep texture quiet · lead with the crown and the favor meter · maintain clear space and 16 px legibility.

**Don't** — recolor or bevel the crown · put gold type/mark on bare cream · overuse crimson · set the wordmark in anything but Bricolage (or its heavy stand-in in rasters) · crowd the mark · add drop shadows or gradients to the logo.

## 9. iOS app assets (App Store)

**This is mobile-first / Capacitor → iOS.** The native **app icon** and **launch/splash** do *not* come from `public/` — they live in the Xcode asset catalog (`ios/App/App/Assets.xcassets`; `ios/` is gitignored, generated by `npx cap add ios` in Phase 1 `01-01`). The web favicon/manifest/og-image are a separate, web-only layer.

- **Sources** (committed, regen via [scripts/gen-ios-assets.sh](../../scripts/gen-ios-assets.sh)):
  - [scripts/assets/app-icon.svg](../../scripts/assets/app-icon.svg) → `assets/icon.png` — **1024×1024, opaque, square, no alpha channel** (App Store rejects alpha; iOS applies its own corner mask — never pre-round).
  - [scripts/assets/splash-light.svg](../../scripts/assets/splash-light.svg) / [splash-dark.svg](../../scripts/assets/splash-dark.svg) → `assets/splash.png` + `assets/splash-dark.png` — **2732×2732**, backgrounds `#FBF6EC` (light) / `#1C1C1E` (dark), matching the `SplashScreen.backgroundColor` and the `LaunchBackground` asset-catalog colors in [capacitor.config.ts](../../capacitor.config.ts).
- **Wire** (after the iOS shell exists): `npx @capacitor/assets generate --ios` → `npx cap sync ios`. This populates `AppIcon.appiconset` (incl. the 1024 marketing icon) and the launch storyboard.
- **App Store submission also needs** (handled in the web layer): a reachable **privacy-policy URL** — [public/privacy.html](../../public/privacy.html) at `/privacy` — and Terms referencing Apple's licensed-application EULA ([public/terms.html](../../public/terms.html)). Target rating **9+/12+**.
- **In-app share/verdict card** (Phase 2) is generated in-app (reuses yapword's share-card pattern) and is governed by `UI-SPEC.md`, not this doc; `og-image.png` is only the *web* link-preview.
